Multi-user role-playing campaign management

A role-playing campaign lab with code for accounts, memberships, invitations and permissions.

Lab published by Samuel, with no client relationship or production delivery.

Implementation evidence based on the public repository, its tests, and development configuration. A current runtime was not verified.

Context and opportunity

Context

The laboratory explores role-playing campaign operations and the access boundaries needed when several people participate.

Modeled opportunity

The scope models how to separate identity, memberships, invitations, and permissions around a campaign. This describes a product exploration, not a confirmed client need.

Implemented scope

  • Authentication and email verification
  • Password recovery
  • External-auth boundaries
  • Campaign CRUD
  • Memberships and invitations
  • Subscription/billing abstractions
  • Next.js client foundation
  • Backend-layer and frontend tests

Capabilities

  • Identity and access
  • Multi-user permissions
  • Campaign management
