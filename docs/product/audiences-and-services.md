# Audiences and services

## Primary audience

Owners and operational decision-makers at small and medium-sized businesses that need to sell, attend to customers, coordinate work, or improve an existing software-supported process.

The initial geographic emphasis is Buenos Aires Province and CABA, without limiting availability elsewhere in Argentina or internationally.

## Priority operating contexts

- Retailers, shops, and convenience stores.
- Restaurants and other gastronomic businesses.
- Passenger transportation, freight, and logistics.
- Service businesses that depend on orders, bookings, or customer coordination.
- IT consultancies needing delivery, integration, maintenance, or advisory capacity.

These are target contexts. The site must not use “specialist in” or “experience in” for a sector unless project evidence supports the claim.

## Secondary audience

- Startup founders and entrepreneurs with a concrete software need.
- Technical partners or consultancies looking for specialized delivery support.
- Organizations needing custom internal tools, integrations, or maintenance.

Recruiters may use the founder profile, but they are not the business site's primary audience.

## Launch services

### SERVICE-WEB — Commercial websites and web applications

**Purpose:** Help a business present, sell, receive requests, or manage a customer-facing process.

Representative outcomes:

- professional websites;
- catalogues and marketplaces;
- orders and reservations;
- customer portals;
- payment-provider integrations;
- business-specific web workflows.

The service spans materially different engagement types rather than one undifferentiated offer:

- a professional commercial site or catalogue primarily communicates and directs enquiries;
- an ordering, reservation, or payment flow supports a transaction and may require provider integrations;
- a portal or custom web application supports authenticated, administrative, or business-specific workflows.

These are discovery categories, not packaged tiers. Typical scope may include diagnosis, content and journey definition, design, development, feasible integrations, testing, launch preparation, and agreed documentation. Brand work, content production, hosting, provider charges, administration features, mobile applications, maintenance, and commercial outcomes are included only when explicitly scoped.

Good fit requires a concrete commercial or operational need and client participation in validation. Existing website, commerce, booking, or business software should be recommended when it meets the need with lower cost and risk.

### SERVICE-WHATSAPP — WhatsApp automation and integrations

**Purpose:** Reduce repetitive attention work and connect conversational entry points with useful business workflows.

Representative outcomes:

- frequent-question flows;
- booking or reservation requests;
- order initiation and follow-up;
- confirmations and notifications;
- payment or external-system integration where providers permit it.

The website must not imply that all payment processing happens inside WhatsApp or that third-party platform capabilities are controlled by FURLANICH.

The service must distinguish a basic contact link from an automated flow, a bot-assisted interaction, an official API/provider integration, and an integration with an existing client system. Automation must define human handover and failure behavior; it is not synonymous with removing people from the process.

Actual scope depends on current WhatsApp/Meta policies, account and template approval where applicable, provider constraints, third-party fees, client data and systems, and integration feasibility. FURLANICH does not control approval, continued availability, message delivery, platform changes, or third-party pricing.

Good fit requires a repeatable flow, defined data, sufficient operational value, and a human owner for exceptions. Low-volume contact may need only a direct link or manual handling; an unstable process should be clarified before it is automated.

### SERVICE-CONSULTING — Software maintenance and IT consulting

**Purpose:** Diagnose, stabilize, integrate, improve, or plan the modernization of existing systems.

Representative outcomes:

- defect investigation;
- dependency and platform updates;
- system integration;
- performance or reliability improvement;
- modernization planning;
- ongoing technical support by agreement.

This service starts from an identifiable existing system. It may include diagnosis, defect reproduction, stabilization, integrations, dependency/framework/platform updates, performance and reliability work, technical-debt or architecture review, modernization planning, agreed remediation, and ongoing support under a separate agreement.

It does not automatically include a full rebuild, a new system, on-call incident response, an SLA, certification, licences, infrastructure, or work owned by another provider. Diagnosis depends on authorized access to code, environments, logs, documentation, suitable data, and people who understand the operation. A supported standard product may be better handled by its vendor; a new system belongs in a development engagement rather than maintenance.

## Offer boundaries

- ERP work and internal management systems may be considered after discovery; they are not a packaged launch product.
- AI may be designed as a bounded capability inside a tailored system or automation, including document processing, an internal agentic workflow, or interpretation of a specific request. It is not a fourth launch service, a unified instant solution, or a default ingredient in every engagement.
- FURLANICH may recommend integration or an existing product when custom development would not serve the client's best interest.
- Entertainment, game-development, and RPG-management work remains possible but should live in the founder profile or Laboratory rather than the primary commercial navigation.
