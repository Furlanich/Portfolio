# Audiences and services

## Primary audience

Owners and operational decision-makers at small and medium-sized businesses that need to sell, attend to customers, coordinate work, or improve an existing software-supported process.

The initial geographic emphasis is Buenos Aires Province and CABA, without limiting availability elsewhere in Argentina or internationally.

## Priority operating contexts

- Retailers, shops, and convenience stores.
- Restaurants and other gastronomic businesses.
- Passenger transportation, freight, and logistics.
- Service businesses that depend on orders, bookings, or customer coordination.
- IT consultancies needing delivery, integration, maintenance, or advisory capacity.

These are target contexts. The site must not use “specialist in” or “experience in” for a sector unless project evidence supports the claim.

## Secondary audience

- Startup founders and entrepreneurs with a concrete software need.
- Technical partners or consultancies looking for specialized delivery support.
- Organizations needing custom internal tools, integrations, or maintenance.

Recruiters may use the founder profile, but they are not the business site's primary audience.

## Launch services

### SERVICE-WEB — Commercial websites and web applications

**Purpose:** Help a business present, sell, receive requests, or manage a customer-facing process.

Representative outcomes:

- professional websites;
- catalogues and marketplaces;
- orders and reservations;
- customer portals;
- payment-provider integrations;
- business-specific web workflows.

### SERVICE-WHATSAPP — WhatsApp automation and integrations

**Purpose:** Reduce repetitive attention work and connect conversational entry points with useful business workflows.

Representative outcomes:

- frequent-question flows;
- booking or reservation requests;
- order initiation and follow-up;
- confirmations and notifications;
- payment or external-system integration where providers permit it.

The website must not imply that all payment processing happens inside WhatsApp or that third-party platform capabilities are controlled by FURLANICH.

### SERVICE-CONSULTING — Software maintenance and IT consulting

**Purpose:** Diagnose, stabilize, integrate, improve, or plan the modernization of existing systems.

Representative outcomes:

- defect investigation;
- dependency and platform updates;
- system integration;
- performance or reliability improvement;
- modernization planning;
- ongoing technical support by agreement.

## Offer boundaries

- ERP work and internal management systems may be considered after discovery; they are not a packaged launch product.
- AI automation may be designed for a specific client need; it is not a unified instant solution.
- FURLANICH may recommend integration or an existing product when custom development would not serve the client's best interest.
- Entertainment, game-development, and RPG-management work remains possible but should live in the founder profile or Laboratory rather than the primary commercial navigation.

## Evidence gaps — OPEN

- Commercial web work has provisional evidence in Busesfy and reservation prototypes, subject to verification and permissions.
- No clearly verified public WhatsApp-automation project is currently inventoried.
- No approved public maintenance/consulting intervention story is currently inventoried.

These gaps must remain visible rather than being filled with fictional client work.
