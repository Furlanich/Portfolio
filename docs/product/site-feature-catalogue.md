# Site feature catalogue

This catalogue records visitor-facing capabilities. It is not an implementation backlog.

## APPROVED launch capabilities

### Navigation and localization

- Multi-page Spanish business experience.
- Equivalent English routes under `/en/`.
- Context-preserving language switch.
- Responsive primary navigation.
- Process anchor on the homepage.
- Footer navigation, contact, founder, legal, and language access.

### Business communication

- Three launch service areas.
- Sector and operational-situation framing.
- Founder-led operating-model explanation.
- Four-step working process.
- Same-day-normal/two-business-day-exception response expectation.

### Project evidence

- Project index with truthful maturity classification.
- Production, laboratory, and prototype concepts.
- Detail pages only where disclosure supports useful content.
- Restricted summaries for confidential production work.
- Calls to inquire about a similar need.

### Founder profile

- Biography.
- Verified professional experience and education.
- Technical capabilities.
- Selected personal or experimental work.
- GitHub, LinkedIn, CV, and contact paths.

### Inquiry

- Required name.
- Required email.
- Optional business name.
- Required message framed as the problem to solve.
- Secondary email, WhatsApp, and phone channels.
- Submission state and understandable error/success feedback.

## PROPOSED launch capabilities

- Service anchors within a single Services page.
- Project-card capability filters if the inventory becomes large enough; no filter is needed for a small launch set.
- Explicit evidence/disclosure labels on project cards.
- Localized canonical, `hreflang`, sitemap, Open Graph, and structured-data metadata.
- A conceptual-image disclosure where generated imagery is used.

## OPEN capabilities and dependencies

- Form provider and anti-spam behavior.
- Consent mechanism and privacy-data lifecycle.
- Final project search/filter behavior.
- Public project demos or video walkthroughs.
- Analytics and conversion measurement.
- Custom domain.
- Blog or resources after launch; excluded from the initial sitemap.

## Explicit non-features at launch

- User accounts.
- Client portal.
- CMS unless later content-maintenance needs justify one.
- Pricing calculator.
- Automated project quotation.
- Automatic locale redirect.
- Public team directory.
- Standalone AI marketing page.
