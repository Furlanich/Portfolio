# Product documentation

Product documentation defines what the FURLANICH website is, who it serves, why each page exists, and what information the site must communicate. It does not define implementation tasks.

## Business foundation

- [Vision and positioning](vision-and-positioning.md): identity, value proposition, operating model, tone, differentiation, and claims.
- [Audiences and services](audiences-and-services.md): priority clients, sectors, launch services, and offer boundaries.
- [Content and localization](content-and-localization.md): Spanish/English structure, voice, terminology, and metadata expectations.
- [Delivery and commercial boundaries](delivery-and-commercial-boundaries.md): approved public process plus proposed engagement, quality, confidentiality, and support boundaries.

## Site definition

- [Information architecture](information-architecture.md): sitemap, routes, navigation, footer, and page relationships.
- [Site feature catalogue](site-feature-catalogue.md): approved and unresolved visitor-facing capabilities.
- [Personal portfolio migration](personal-portfolio-migration.md): what remains, moves, changes, or becomes secondary.
- [Project evidence governance](project-evidence.md): approved three-axis taxonomy, disclosure controls, publication lifecycle, and Initiative 4 closure.
- [Item-level project inventory](projects/index.md): candidate records, permission matrices, explicit publication states, repository-discovery disposition, ordering policy, and readiness matrices.
- [Projects and evidence experience](projects/experience.md): accepted index, card, detail, imagery, bilingual-language, accessibility, performance, and visual-QA decisions.

## Page specifications

- [`PAGE-HOME`](pages/home.md)
- [`PAGE-SERVICES`](pages/services.md)
- [`PAGE-PROJECTS` and `PAGE-PROJECT-DETAIL`](pages/projects.md)
- [`PAGE-STUDIO` and `PAGE-FOUNDER`](pages/studio-and-founder.md)
- [`PAGE-CONTACT` and `PAGE-PRIVACY`](pages/contact-and-privacy.md)

`PAGE-NOT-FOUND` is defined at the responsibility level in the information architecture because no detailed content design has been approved.

Initiative 3 makes `PAGE-SERVICES` implementation-ready in Spanish and English, including stable anchors, service-specific scope and boundaries, truthful evidence status, contextual CTAs, design/accessibility requirements, and an explicit readiness matrix. Future evidence and contractual terms remain governed by their owning records rather than blocking the approved page.

Initiative 4 makes the Projects product and design decisions complete but does not authorize route implementation: every candidate fails at least one evidence or permission gate. The approved inventory-sensitive structure rejects empty groups and launch filters, keeps `HOME-PROOF` unchanged, and requires at least one `READY` or `READY-SUMMARY-ONLY` item before the Projects route launches.
