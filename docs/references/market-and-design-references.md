# Market, evidence, and design references

References record what informed prior discussion. They are not product requirements, endorsements, or proof that competitor claims are accurate. Reassess them before using time-sensitive conclusions.

- **WAPP Software Factory:** [wapp.com.ar](https://www.wapp.com.ar/) — named as an example of confident professional presentation. It uses broad custom-development, consulting, AI, and infrastructure service language plus client logos.
- **Simple Solutions:** [simplesolutions.com.ar](https://www.simplesolutions.com.ar/) — named as a professionalism reference. It presents one focused operational product, concrete capabilities, customer identities, geographic reach, press, and demo-oriented contact.

## Reference-use rule

- Extract a principle, not a copied layout or claim.
- Verify a source is still current before relying on it.
- Do not copy competitor metrics, testimonials, imagery, or distinctive creative expression.
- Record any future reference that materially influences an approved design decision.
