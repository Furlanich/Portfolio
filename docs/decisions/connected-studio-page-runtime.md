# Connected studio pages and definitive project-detail retirement

## Context

The owner approved Revision 5 of [DESIGN-SPF-V1](../design/services-projects-footer-v1.md), its linked bilingual copy and the bounded exceptions in [RFC-SPF-REDESIGN-V1](../rfcs/services-projects-footer-redesign-v1.md), then explicitly authorized ADE v2 planning and independent review on 2026-09-30. Production implementation is outside this session. Task 1 of the execution plan packages these accepted records for a human-merged Governance PR before implementation.

## Decision

- Keep Spanish-root/English-`/en/` static export, trailing slashes and project-site base-path support. Publish the complete permitted GRS and The-System dossiers on the Projects indexes; definitively retire all six localized detail destinations without redirects or compatibility routes. Preserve internal evidence IDs, provenance and immutable history. MPC remains educational/group/fictional context on Founder with its approved external source.
- Extend optional direct installed Three.js to Services and Projects only. Semantic HTML, conceptual captions, capability vocabulary, direct actions and matched static artwork load independently of WebGL. No new 3D/motion framework or Brag video runtime is required.
- Give these routes their own controller and scene modules. The approved viewport-wide field has 16 nodes/33 paths wide and eight/13 compact, upright localized words, slow capped idle rotation and immediate reversible scroll/velocity response. Home's demand-only controller remains unchanged.
- Use one renderer per active route, cancellable asynchronous activation, complete disposal, preference/capability gates, session-persistent Pause and context-loss suppression for the connected routes. No engine fetch for known static preferences. Paused/hidden/Footer-dominated scenes stop their scheduled work; focused Pause survives fallback as an inactive localized static-background control.
- Adopt the route-specific loading/performance/accessibility limits in DESIGN-SPF-V1. Prototype and software-GPU evidence do not establish hardware, phone, CWV or screen-reader acceptance. Count the complete optional production chunk closure and gate physical-device performance before accepting live enhancement.
- Use the shared Azure Footer with the whole protected foreground mark and complete static watermark. Preserve real channels, equivalent locale navigation and global demonstration disclosure; Contact remains local simulation with zero transmission.

## Scope of supersession

This is a **partial extension**, not replacement of the Home runtime ADR. It supersedes only its prohibition on WebGL outside Home for the two named routes under the new contract. Its Home rendering, mark protection, established gates and scene choreography remain authoritative for Home. The exact product/design exceptions are the bounded table in DESIGN-SPF-V1; earlier ADR files remain immutable. No hosting, backend, publication-permission or Contact activation decision changes.

## Rationale and consequences

Atlas makes the commercial hierarchy legible; complete dossiers reduce navigation while retaining all evidence limitations. Connected backgrounds extend the approved Home grammar without copying Aureon. Separate controllers avoid changing Home's tested demand-only semantics. Low-rate idle motion has an energy cost, addressed through cadence caps, lifecycle suspension, reduced geometry and static equivalence. Removing old URLs sacrifices deep-link reachability by explicit owner choice; clean exports and negative route tests prevent accidental residual deployment.

## Alternatives rejected

Retaining detail routes/redirects, mandatory live graphics on every phone, reusing Home's zero-idle scheduler, decorative logo sculpture, new GSAP/R3F/video dependencies and a fixed autoplay narrative were rejected or unnecessary. Three distinct prototype directions and skill conflicts are recorded in the [design review](../reviews/services-projects-footer-design-2026-09-30/index.md).
