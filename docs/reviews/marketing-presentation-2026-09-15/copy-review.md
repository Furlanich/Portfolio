# Public copy review — current text, not replacement copy

[Main audit](index.md) · [Findings](findings.md)

Coverage: both locales' hero, all homepage sections, three complete Services sections and closing principles/boundaries, Projects index/three cards/three details, Studio, Founder, Contact normal/error/success states, shared navigation/footer, and Privacy presentation. Excerpts below identify the important issues; unchanged accurate lines are not rewritten for novelty. The quoted copy comes from locale modules and was cross-checked against rendered text. Screen-reader-only labels are not classified as visible defects.

Every direction is PROPOSED and requires the appropriate page owner's copy approval. No polished replacement is adopted here. Tags describe presentation, not proof of dishonesty. UNSUPPORTED CLAIM identifies an unsupported interpretation or current-mode promise explicitly described in the row; it does not invalidate approved founder facts or permitted project statements.

## What should survive editing

- Concrete buyer situations and tangible examples in Home/Services.
- “No hace falta…” reassurance, expressed naturally in each language.
- Exact approved founder experience/education dates and employment limits.
- Project maturity, group authorship, fictional context, current-runtime limits and conceptual labels.
- WhatsApp provider/feasibility dependencies and human handoff.
- Four appropriate Contact fields, visible labels, preserved input on failure and truthful simulation confirmation.
- Direct technical responsibility as a practical benefit, stated fewer times.

No generic, repetitive or awkward phrase is evidence of AI authorship. The issue is how the writing sounds and functions for the buyer, not an assertion about its origin.
