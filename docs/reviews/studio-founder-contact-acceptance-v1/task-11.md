What now exists
- `ConnectedStudioEnhancement.tsx`: one client leaf for all five routes. It dynamically imports the gated entry once, builds the route profile (`getConnectedProfile`) from the viewport, hands it to the renderer (`createConnectedScene`, `resize(…, profile)`) and to the controller (`profile`, `profileKit: entry.connectedProfileKit`), and rebuilds an Atlas profile only when the tier or the viewport size changes (not on content growth, so a growing form does not reset the interaction). Words come from `resolveNodeWord`: the vocabulary for Services and Projects, the bound ids intersected with `profile.wordNodeIds` for the new routes.
- Cached input: `labelLayer.measure()` reads, in the one batched pass, label sizes, the viewport and every `[data-connected-opaque-plate]`/`[data-connected-reading-mask]` box with its `data-connected-clearance`. Labels avoid a plate as drawn and a scrim with its clearance; new excitation is excluded 8 px beyond a plate and clearance + 8 px beyond a scrim. The pointer handler is one passive `pointermove` listener that reads the event and the cached boxes only: mouse only (touch and pen keep native scrolling), nothing over a link, button, summary, field, label or form, and a pointer that rests over an excluded area is forgotten (sampler reset), so it carries no velocity on re-entry. Updates are coalesced to one per animation frame. Scroll deltas feed the interaction only after a wheel, touch or scroll-key gesture (typing and scrolling in a field are not gestures), never for an oversized delta, and never after an anchor, history, page-show or resize event.
- Form signal (`form[data-inquiry-phase]`): delegated focus and a `MutationObserver` on the phase attribute suspend the drawing by pausing the controller (a pause clears stimulation and holds zero callbacks; the last frame stays visible). The suspension lifts only on the next eligible background input after focus has left and the phase is not `submitting`; the visitor's own Pause is kept.
- A hidden tab, the Footer and a Pause forget the pointer. The static-background label is hidden by the host while the control is shown, for every route.
- Diagnostics (non-production only): `layoutReads`, `formSuspended`, `pointerActive`, `profile`.
