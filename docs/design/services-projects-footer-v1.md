# Services, Projects and Footer — connected studio v1

**APPROVED — Revision 5, 2026-09-30.** After inspecting the refined field and linked written specification/copy, the owner said: “Perfect, you can now proceed with ADE v2 planning and independent review.” This closes the design approval gate for Revision 5 and its exact linked ES/EN copy, including the bounded supersessions below. Revision 4 and earlier candidates remain historical exploration. Production files and old project routes remain unchanged; design approval authorizes planning, not implementation or merge.

Make the studio's offer easy to buy, its work easy to examine, and its conclusion memorable. Preserve the approved Sky Chart Home/App Bar identity: Azure, Bone, deep chart backgrounds, Instrument Sans, Plex Mono, plotting details and legible foreground plates. Use Aureon's confidence, image scale and continuity as references; do not reproduce its composition, claims or interactions.

The [review package](../reviews/services-projects-footer-design-2026-09-30/index.md) records the three alternatives, rendered references, skill critique and observed prototype checks. Exact approved public copy lives in the SPF-V1 blocks of [Services](../product/pages/services.md#spf-v1-proposed-services-copy), [Projects](../product/pages/projects.md#spf-v1-proposed-projects-copy-and-inline-presentation) and [information architecture](../product/information-architecture.md#spf-v1-proposed-footer-copy-and-route-retirement). ## Services hierarchy and wording

The page sequence is introduction → asymmetric catalogue → three service chapters → working boundaries → shared Footer. The catalogue makes the web family dominant: one large card on the left and two supporting cards stacked on the right. Each card is a genuine native anchor to its chapter. There is no category filter, technology wall or seven-card equal-weight catalogue.

Each chapter contains its category, problem/outcome headline, useful delivery, engagement fit, technical accountability, evidence limitation and localized Contact action. This structure lets business buyers understand what changes for them without translating framework names. APIs/backend are capabilities within applications and integrations. Consulting is a way to scope or resolve a technical problem; maintenance/support is a scoped continuity engagement, not a new implied SLA.

Keep all approved commercial, provider-cost, authorization, security and AI boundaries in the owning records. Never imply a permanent delivery team, autonomous AI decisions, production client results, fixed prices, free diagnostics or guaranteed response times. The GRS evidence link reaches its complete index dossier; automation and maintenance do not borrow GRS proof for unverified delivery claims.

After each service's concise scope/fit facts, visibly retain its approved compressed boundary paragraph and a same-page working-boundaries link before evidence/Contact. After the three working-principle paragraphs, retain the approved shared working agreement, AI/ERP scope and complete **Límites comerciales / Commercial boundaries** text verbatim. They remain ordinary visible content, with no accordion, tooltip or reveal. The proposed short catalogue/delivery wording does not replace these disclosures.

## Scene integration, depth and progression

One decorative canvas per active route, fixed behind the semantic page and masked by content scrims. Stop/hide the environment as the opaque Footer takes over. Both pages use a field spread across the visible viewport rather than a cluster moving into one side lane. Wide/tablet uses 16 nodes and 33 connections; compact uses eight nodes and 13 connections. Each of the eight capability words appears twice in the wide field and once in compact. A readable dispersed arrangement and bounded local travel prevent collisions/offscreen escape; density is deliberately short of all-to-all wiring. Avoid one canvas per card or service.

Revision 5 retains faceted Azure nodes, small Bone light cores, clear rings, curved paths and brighter growing segments. Wide mode has a faint complete connection skeleton and a second tilted orbit; compact simplifies both. Gentle directional lighting and fog separate nearer/larger nodes from smaller distant ones. Connections are batched low-poly geometry, allowing greater density without one draw call per path. No bloom pipeline, film, model download, reflective HDR texture, logo sculpture or particle storm.

The candidate reference uses node radius 0.22, primary ring radius 0.39 and path radius 0.025 in scene units, with depth-scaled size and a lighter compact variant. These describe visible proportions, not production acceptance tests. Preserve perceived clarity and depth; exact geometry may be optimized if the approved rendering remains equivalent.

Connection progress uses actual page geometry from scroll position zero to the start of the Footer handoff, recomputed for resize and locale wrapping. The first scroll event starts movement and connection growth while the introduction is still visible; there is no wait for the first service/article. All paths complete by the Footer handoff. Scrolling upward reverses connection growth; the current pose retargets fluidly without replaying prior chapters. A roughly 48%-viewport reading line still supplies the foreground chapter accent, independently of whole-page network progress. No discrete side-lane flips remain.

While visible and unpaused, nodes/rings rotate slowly at approximately 1–1.4 degrees per second; label words stay upright. Idle positions do not wander. From the first scroll, a bounded scroll-velocity impulse accelerates local node movement/rotation and starts connection growth. Use approximately 70ms progress damping and 240ms impulse decay; rapid movement returns smoothly to gentle rotation after input ends. Depth travel is bounded and connections follow their endpoints. No camera roll, fly-through, large zoom, or pointer-driven camera. Rapid jumps, anchors and resize recompute the current state without replaying the document.

No scroll interception, pinned text, scroll-jacking or hidden reading content. The explicit owner request supersedes the former zero-idle-frame contract: visible eligible scenes continue low-rate node rotation. Target 30fps ambient wide / 20fps ambient compact and up to 60fps during active scroll; gate actual hardware timing and reduce/fall back where necessary. Paused, hidden, rejected, reduced-motion and Footer-dominated states stop all callbacks/drawing. The graphic is a background experience; text is available immediately and independently.

Every node has a localized upright word associated with it. Implement labels as projected DOM text or an equivalently legible low-cost technique; they are non-interactive and decorative. Keep label bounds inside the viewport, prevent overlapping labels and suppress labels occluded by foreground copy/plates. Reading masks protect H1, body, captions and controls. A normal semantic capability legend provides the same vocabulary without canvas or JavaScript. Labels are not affected by hover and do not rotate with mesh geometry.

## Motion and interaction contract

Brag informed the pacing: gently rotating labeled field at entry → connection response from the first scroll → denser network through inspectable highlights → completed network and branded conclusion. Brag's skill is a launch-video workflow; its storyboard advice is adapted here. Do not install Hyperframes, add an autoplay soundtrack or run a fixed video timeline on the website.

| Interaction | Target behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Scroll | Native page scroll; immediate velocity-responsive field and reversible connection growth; active chapter border changes softly, no content reveal |
| Idle | Slow rotation only, with capped ambient cadence; positions/words remain steady; visible Pause stops it |
| Catalogue hover | Fine-pointer only: at most 3px upward movement, brighter border and 3px action-arrow translation; 160–220ms explicit-property transitions |
| Pointer highlight | Restrained radial light inside catalogue card; no layout shift, pointer-driven camera or coarse-pointer dependency |
| Dossier hover | Border emphasis; the full article does not tilt or move |
| Artwork hover | Fine-pointer-only image scale up to 1.018 within its reserved crop, approximately 260ms; caption stays still |
| Links/buttons | Existing Home/App Bar conventions; color/outline feedback, visible focus, no mandatory press-scale treatment |
| Keyboard | Native links; catalogue anchors use sufficient sticky-header offset; focus visibility provides action affordance without requiring hover |
| Pause/resume | Bilingual button in the hero action area; at least 44px target; `aria-pressed` reports pause; frozen scene does not block content |

Reserve the Pause control's slot before optional enhancement so adding it cannot move headings or actions. Show it only for an active enhancement. If reduced motion, failure or context loss occurs while it holds keyboard focus, preserve the element and focus, change the label to “Fondo estático” / “Static background” and mark it inactive with `aria-disabled`; do not remove a focused control. Hide the unused reserved slot when appropriate without collapsing its space. Never place Pause over mobile body copy.

Pause persists across locale/route navigation within the browsing session. Resume computes the current scroll state rather than catching up through an unseen animation. Scroll may still change a semantic chapter accent while paused, but must not advance the canvas.

## Responsive and mobile behavior

| Range | Layout and scene |
| --- | --- |
| Wide, at least 1024px | Asymmetric catalogue; alternating two-column Services; two-column dossier cover/body; dispersed 16-node/33-path field with clearer depth; desktop Footer navigation |
| Tablet, 768–1023px | Same reading order; tighter gutters/plate padding, restrained depth/travel; collapse any layout that cannot retain readable columns at zoom |
| Compact, below 768px | Single column in document order; natural chapter height; no alternating grid rearrangement; short approximately 160px visual breathing areas; stacked dossiers/limits; wrapped Footer utilities |

At 320px, no horizontal document overflow, clipped labels or compressed tap targets. At 200% zoom, reflow and readable content outrank preserving a wide composition. Support portrait/landscape and mobile browser chrome changes. Do not disable zoom or introduce scroll traps.

On capable phones use eight labeled nodes, 13 batched low-poly paths, one ring per node, fewer distant points, smaller spatial travel, a steady camera and capped DPR. Ambient rotation is capped at 20fps. No second orbit, video or bloom. The simplified scene must remain visually connected and legible, not a cloud of unrelated dots. Labels are clamped/collision-checked and occluded behind reading content; do not shrink below approximately 11px to fit more words. Compact live motion must meet the same content, lifecycle and measured frame gates. If it cannot, show the complete static alternative without asking the visitor to diagnose capability.

## Accessibility, preferences and fallbacks

All service/project content, links, maturity labels, images/captions, limitations and Footer utilities are semantic server HTML. JavaScript, canvas and animation never carry unique information. Canvas is decorative, `aria-hidden`, unfocusable and pointer-inert; all meaningful interactions are DOM links/buttons. Maintain one H1, heading order, article/section landmarks and 44px action targets.

| Condition | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Reduced motion at entry | Complete static environment; do not fetch scene engine; remove movement, animated zoom/highlights and hover transforms |
| Reduced motion changes while open | Cancel frames, dispose live resources, show static artwork; preserve focused control as described above |
| Save-Data | Static environment and no optional scene fetch |
| No WebGL2 / software renderer / rejected capability | Static environment; no repeated initialization attempts or warning modal |
| Optional module/render failure | Complete content plus static background; no unhandled promise or page error; retry never required to read |
| WebGL context loss | Dispose and use static for the browsing session, including locale/route changes; no repeated context creation |
| Hidden tab, offscreen environment or Footer dominance | Stop pending frames; resume current state only when visible and eligible |
| No JavaScript | Complete rendered page with static environment and usable localized/direct links |
| Forced colors | Remove decorative environment/watermark; use system colors, visible borders/links and focus |

Use matched desktop/compact static posters showing the stronger graph composition, with a CSS chart/gradient final fallback if an image fails. Prefer one shared poster family with coherent Services/Projects variants rather than different visual identities. Prototype SVG art proves static composition/content access; final exact-pose posters still require rendered validation. Fade optional canvas over static artwork without changing dimensions and without a bright flash.

Do not claim WCAG conformance from axe alone. Verify screen-reader reading order, headings, visible focus, Pause announcements, anchor destinations, language change, contact links, contrast over every scene state and actual touch use manually.

## Loading and performance contract

Retain direct installed Three.js. Reuse proven Home primitives only where their scope and implementation match this design; do not force Home choreography into a generic abstraction. No new motion/3D library is required. This proposal explicitly extends route scope beyond the Home-only ADR; that extension requires the [governance proposal](../rfcs/services-projects-footer-redesign-v1.md), not an implicit inheritance.

Critical HTML, self-hosted fonts and current above-fold layout load first. Static art appears before canvas. Gate optional dynamic import after initial load/critical paint and proximity/visibility, preferences and coarse capability checks. Do not import Three for known static preference paths. A lightweight capability probe may reject WebGL before the engine; clean up its temporary context before creating the single live renderer. It must not steal or duplicate a live context. Avoid preloading optional engine, below-fold project images or every poster variant.

| Target gate | Proposed ceiling / acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Optional route scene JS | At most 120KiB Brotli incremental engine/runtime chunk closure; reuse shared engine cache; count actual production chunks, not prototype source size |
| Scene draw calls | At most 28 desktop / 18 compact; Revision 5 batches dense paths instead of allocating a draw call per connection; actual observations belong in the review evidence |
| DPR | At most 1.5 desktop/tablet / 1.25 compact, lowered if needed |
| Scene poster | At most 150KiB wide / 80KiB compact; correct responsive selection and intrinsic sizing |
| Frame cost | Hardware p95 at most 20ms on the repository's agreed desktop and constrained-phone profiles; software preview is not acceptance |
| Main-thread work | Scene-related interaction task below 50ms; no layout read/write churn on every pointer/scroll event |
| Experience | No enhancement-induced layout shift; LCP at most 2.5s and INP at most 200ms on the established acceptance profile |
| Lifecycle | One active renderer; disposed GPU resources/listeners/observers after navigation; capped visible ambient loop; zero paused/hidden/Footer-dominated frames |

These are proposed route-specific gates based on Home's established ceilings, not a claim that Home's allowance already applies to Services/Projects. If a scene exceeds them, optimize or fall back; content clarity and timing take priority over desktop geometry density. Record actual bundle bytes, image bytes, device/browser/GPU, collection method and before/after measurements. A named physical constrained-phone profile is required before runtime acceptance; it cannot be replaced by SwiftShader or desktop emulation.

## Verification required after implementation

This section is acceptance design, not an implementation sequence. The later ADE v2 plan must identify the first failing behavior and verified RED → GREEN → REFACTOR cycle for each behavior-changing task. Do not assert CSS utility names or scene-coordinate constants as behavioral proof. Pure visual requirements use this specification, rendered inspection and targeted stable visual regression.

- Unit/contract coverage: localized page/story data, whitelist/permission boundaries, known-fragment mapping, removed detail route generation, scene capability/preference/failure/pause/lifecycle transitions and clean export absence.
- Playwright: complete ES/EN reading content before enhancement and without JavaScript; anchors and sticky offset; source/direct contact links; locale + fragments; keyboard/focus; dynamic reduced motion; Pause/resume; context loss/module failure; actual route transitions; no live canvas after disposal; static root and `/Portfolio` export.
- Responsive: 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px; long English copy, 200% zoom, landscape, touch, no hover, mobile chrome; real capable and constrained phones.
- Cross-browser: Chromium, Firefox, desktop WebKit where available and physical iOS Safari/Android Chrome for relevant live/fallback behavior. An unavailable environment remains explicitly unverified.

- Visual QA: Services hierarchy, whole dossier readability, concept/capability labels, field distribution, path/node strength, depth, continuous local movement, mobile scene, static posters, Footer mark clear space and compatibility with every shared-page host. Taste preflight and post-implementation critique remain subordinate to this specification after owner approval.
- Motion/performance: slow rotation at rest, first-scroll acceleration/growth before any chapter, sufficient complete connections at Footer handoff, reversible scroll, fast scroll/anchor jumps, interruption, pause/hidden/Footer stop, upright/occlusion-safe labels, full route/locale disposal, actual bundle/poster bytes, hardware ambient/active frame timing/CWV and clean static-export/base-path checks.
