# Design documentation

The homepage-foundation, complete commercial-homepage, Services, Projects/Evidence, Studio/Founder, and Contact visual, responsive, accessibility, and motion baselines are approved. The complete whole-site design system remains intentionally unresolved. These documents distinguish approved page requirements, the existing implementation, and later OPEN decisions.

- [Visual language](visual-language.md): current palette/type/component baseline; approved homepage, Services, Projects, Studio/Founder, and Contact composition and control constraints; and open broader design decisions.
- [Interaction, responsive behavior, and accessibility](interaction-responsive-accessibility.md): existing behaviors, known problems, approved homepage, Services, Projects, Studio/Founder, and Contact baselines, and broader open interaction and conformance decisions.
- [Market and design references](../references/market-and-design-references.md): external examples and research; references are not requirements.

Future visual decisions must be persisted here before they are treated as implementation constraints. The approved baselines are sufficient for their named page scopes, including the Contact form states; they do not approve every later page or component treatment.
