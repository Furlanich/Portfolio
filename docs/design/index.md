# Design documentation

The homepage-foundation, complete commercial-homepage, complete Services-page, and Projects/Evidence visual, responsive, accessibility, and motion baselines are approved. The complete whole-site design system remains intentionally unresolved. These documents distinguish approved page requirements, the existing implementation, and later OPEN decisions.

- [Visual language](visual-language.md): current palette/type/component baseline; approved homepage, Services, and Projects composition and image constraints; and open broader design decisions.
- [Interaction, responsive behavior, and accessibility](interaction-responsive-accessibility.md): existing behaviors, known problems, approved homepage, Services, and Projects baselines, and broader open interaction and conformance decisions.
- [Market and design references](../references/market-and-design-references.md): external examples and research; references are not requirements.

Future visual decisions must be persisted here before they are treated as implementation constraints. The approved baselines are sufficient for the implemented homepage and Services experience and for a future evidence-unblocked Projects implementation; they do not approve every later page or component treatment.
