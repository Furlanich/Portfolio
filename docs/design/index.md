# Design documentation

The homepage-foundation, complete commercial-homepage, and complete Services-page visual, responsive, accessibility, and motion baselines are approved. The complete whole-site design system remains intentionally unresolved. These documents distinguish approved page requirements, the existing implementation, and later OPEN decisions.

- [Visual language](visual-language.md): current palette/type/component baseline, approved communication principles, image constraints, and open design decisions.
- [Interaction, responsive behavior, and accessibility](interaction-responsive-accessibility.md): existing behaviors, known problems, the approved homepage and Services baselines, and broader open interaction and conformance decisions.
- [Market and design references](../references/market-and-design-references.md): external examples and research; references are not requirements.

Future visual decisions must be persisted here before they are treated as implementation constraints. The approved baselines are sufficient for planning `HOME-HERO`, the seven later commercial sections, and the complete `PAGE-SERVICES`; they do not approve every later page or component treatment.
