# FURLANICH Product Context

This glossary defines the project-specific language used throughout the product documentation. It describes product concepts, not implementation.

## Business and people

**FURLANICH**:
The commercial identity for the founder-led software studio.
_Avoid_: FURLANICH Solutions, Samuel's portfolio

**Founder**:
Samuel Furlanich, the public face and directly accountable technical lead of FURLANICH.
_Avoid_: employee, agency representative

**Collaborator**:
A specialist who may join a project when its scope requires additional expertise. The existence or identity of collaborators is not a primary sales claim.
_Avoid_: permanent team member, employee

**Prospective client**:
A business owner, operational decision-maker, organization, or technical partner evaluating whether FURLANICH can address a need.
_Avoid_: recruiter, visitor persona

## Website areas

**Business site**:
The primary commercial experience that explains services, evidence, working process, studio identity, and contact paths.
_Avoid_: portfolio, personal site

**Founder profile**:
The secondary area containing Samuel's biography, verified professional history, skills, education, selected work, CV, and professional links.
_Avoid_: about-me homepage, team page

**Project evidence**:
Material that helps a prospective client evaluate relevance and credibility, with its maturity and disclosure limits stated accurately.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, success story when no outcome is verified

## Project maturity

**Production solution**:
A solution used in a real operating context. Public disclosure remains limited by client permission and confidentiality.
_Avoid_: productive solution, case study unless sufficient evidence is approved

**Laboratory solution**:
A FURLANICH-owned exploration or demonstration built to investigate a commercially relevant problem or capability.
_Avoid_: client work, production solution

**Functional prototype**:
A working but non-production implementation that demonstrates specific behavior and states its limitations.
_Avoid_: product, production system, client solution

## Conversion

**Inquiry**:
A prospective client's initial structured request through the contact form or a secondary contact channel.
_Avoid_: lead when addressing the visitor, support ticket
